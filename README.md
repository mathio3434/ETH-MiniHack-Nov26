# ETH-MiniHack-Nov26 by Optiver

# Background
On Friday 6th November, I had an opportunity to visit the Mini Hackathon by Optiver & ETH Entrepreneurship CLub in Zurich. Before the event, I have spent the week doing my best to understand the basics of the market-making and in this repository I would like to elaborate on my challenges during the event, while not directly giving any hints to ensure the fair competition for the next hackathons.

# Challenge Structure
The hackathon took place for around 5 hours of testing time and one final evaluation round, during which the winner was estimated. During the preparation round, we were testing dfferent stratagies and ideas. We were trading a universe of 6 Tech stocks (no options, etfs or futures), although it is not the fundamentals that mattered behind (as prices were not a representation of a real market price), but the patterns and behaviour of other participants. The teams of 3-4 members were formed and given access to a cloud kernel to run the research and ideas using Optibook. (You can find any further information online, as I did before the competition, difference is, every challange differes in its structure and limitations)
